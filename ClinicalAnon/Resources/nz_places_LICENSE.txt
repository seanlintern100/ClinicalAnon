NZ Place Names Data Sources
============================

This file contains New Zealand place names compiled from:

1. NZ Localities Dataset (GitHub Gist by wilr)
   Source: https://gist.github.com/wilr/815715
   ~1,500 NZ suburbs, districts, and regions

2. SimpleMaps NZ Cities Database
   Source: https://simplemaps.com/data/nz-cities
   License: MIT
   93 prominent NZ cities

3. Manual additions of well-known NZ places (national parks,
   tourist destinations, Canterbury/Auckland suburbs)

Original LINZ Gazetteer data is licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) by Land Information New Zealand.
